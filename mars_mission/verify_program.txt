Verifying the program — Missions 1–4

1. Break at the two key points and run:
   break finished
   break mission4_done
   continue

2. First breakpoint hits at `finished` (right after Missions 1-3's loop
   ends, before any STRs run). Continue past it:
   continue

3. Second breakpoint hits at `mission4_done` — this is after every STR
   for report AND every STR for corrected has executed, right before
   the subroutine returns. This is the correct place to check memory,
   not at `finished` itself (breakpoints stop BEFORE a line runs, so
   checking memory at `finished` will show all zeros — that's expected,
   not a bug).

4. Check report (8 words):
   x/8dw &report

   Expected:
   109  -18  42  6
   3    1    3   0

   (sum, min, max, positive_count, negative_count, zero_count,
   unsafe_count, unused)

5. Check corrected (10 words — report is only 8, don't stop early):
   x/10dw &corrected

   Expected:
   12  -5  0   27
   30  -10 7   30
   -2  15

   (each reading clamped to [-10, 30])

If either output doesn't match, don't guess — step line-by-line from
`finished` with `next` and check `i r` (registers) or `x` (memory)
after each store to find exactly which one diverges first.
